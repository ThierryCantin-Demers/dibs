2| no inventory at <root>/none.toml
2| Write one with:  dibs --check <host> --write

== dibs --machines  (with a shared registry under your own)
-> exit 0
1|    box-a    dibs@box-a  [yours]
1|    box-b    box-b.local  (no measurements)  [yours]
1|    team-box dibs@team-box  [shared]
2|   A 'default =' line in the inventory is no longer read: a call names its machine.

== dibs --forget team-box
-> exit 2
2| dibs: 'team-box' comes from the shared registry, not from <root>/machines.toml
2|   Nothing here can remove it. Give it an entry of your own to override it.
